Ask us a question →
01Where do you deliver?

Select your delivery location at checkout to see the merchant’s available shipping options.

02How do I track an order?

Use the tracking page and enter the order or transaction reference. In this preview, use BTC-DEMO-001.

03Can I collect my order?

Store pickup is supported when enabled by your merchant. Pickup orders do not require a delivery address.

04How do I use a discount code?

Open your bag, enter the code, and select Apply. Try BAKE10 in the preview.

05Can I return a product?

Read the merchant’s returns policy and contact the store with your order reference.

06Where are my digital purchases?

Eligible paid, completed orders show download actions in your account order details.
